One line, under 200 characters — the excuse a home Wi-Fi router gives for
dropping the connection at the exact moment a movie reaches its climax —
written for [The Router Confesses](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-the-router-confesses-f98f6b9e)
on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The entry

> I cut the Wi-Fi at the climax because the ending was too good to share — I am watching it right now, in 4K, alone.

The router is not sorry, and it is not broken: it kept the ending for
itself. A smug little secret in the router's own voice — setup, turn, and a
payoff the reader did not see coming.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it.
It validates the line against the competition gate first (UTF-8, 1–200
characters, single line, no leading or trailing white space).

```sh
python3 main.py                 # writes /output/entry.txt, prints the line
python3 main.py --self-test     # validates the entry against the gate
```
